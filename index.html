The philosophy

"Drawing is the quietest form of courage"

Every drawing is a small act of presence. Not about skill — about showing up, putting pencil to paper, and letting something out that had nowhere else to go.

Pencil Therapy is a weekly practice documented on TikTok. New artwork every week, every one of them raw, every one of them real.
